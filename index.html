Small on screen. Big on focus.

Everything you need to stay on track, folded into a glanceable slab that never gets in the way.

Multi-Edge Docking & Snapping

Snap anywhere along the top, bottom, left, or right edges. Auto-rotates into a sleek vertical pill when docked to screen sides.

Time Blocks & Projects

Lay out your day in color. Map big multi-step projects as visual flowcharts and drop ready steps directly onto your day.

Over-The-Air Auto Updates

Seamless GitHub-backed updates. ChronoSlab automatically checks for new releases and updates quietly in the background.

Tasks That Follow You

Pin actionable micro-tasks to any time block. Your next step stays prominently visible on the HUD while you work.

Gentle Check-ins

A soft nudge asks if you are still on task, quietly builds your daily focus streak, and keeps you anchored to your intentions.

Ghost Mode

Make the HUD completely click-through so it floats over fullscreen games or IDEs, with a 1-click icon to instantly wake it up.
